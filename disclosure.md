# Affiliate Disclosure

*Last updated: 25 September 2026*

**Vibezzzz earns a commission on some of the links it shows. What it recommends is never affected by
that.**

Vibezzzz is free, shows no advertising, and sells nothing. It is funded by affiliate links: when you
choose to open a title on a streaming service or in a store, that service may pay us a referral fee
if you subscribe, rent, or buy. It costs you nothing extra, and the price you pay is the same as if
you had gone there directly.

This disclosure is also shown inside the app, in the header of the sheet that lists where to watch,
read, or play — the screen where you would actually tap such a link.

## What determines the results you see

Two things, and only these two:

1. **How well a title matches the vibe you described.**
2. **The title's own quality and recognisability.**

Commission rates, payout amounts, and which services we have a commercial relationship with are
**excluded from ranking entirely**. That is enforced in the app's design: the ranking code has no
access to commercial data of any kind, and a service we earn nothing from competes on exactly equal
terms with one that pays us well. We will not add revenue-weighted ranking later — it is ruled out on
purpose, not deferred.

## What a commercial relationship does affect

Being straightforward about this is the point of the page:

- **The order of services within one group of the availability sheet.** If three services all stream a
  title, a service we have a partnership with may be listed above one we do not. Every service that
  carries the title is still shown, with its correct name and logo, whether we earn from it or not.
- **The link itself.** A partnered link routes through the affiliate network so the visit can be
  credited. It goes to the same destination.

It does not affect which titles appear, their order, their scores, their descriptions, or the content
warnings and availability badges shown on them. Those come from the catalog, not from any commercial
arrangement.

## What is never shown

The app displays **no prices**, from any source. A catalog rebuilt weekly cannot keep prices accurate,
and a stale price is worse than none — so you get availability only: whether a title streams, rents,
sells, or is free with advertising. Check the price on the service itself.

## What the destination learns about you

An affiliate link carries a short tracking label so a referral can be credited. It contains which part
of the app you tapped from, one broad mood category from a fixed list, and the app version. It never
contains your search text, any identifier for you or your device, or a timestamp. The
[privacy policy](../privacy/) sets this out in full.

## Programmes

We participate, or intend to participate, in affiliate programmes run by streaming services, book
retailers, and game stores, directly and through the affiliate networks Impact, Rakuten Advertising,
and CJ Affiliate. Which programmes are active changes over time; a link's destination always shows you
who you are dealing with before you commit to anything.

## Why this page exists

United States law — the FTC's Endorsement Guides, 16 CFR Part 255 — requires that a material
connection between a recommender and the things it recommends be disclosed clearly and conspicuously.
Both app stores require the same. We would disclose it regardless: a recommendation you cannot trust
is not worth making.
