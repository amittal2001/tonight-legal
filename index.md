## How it works

You describe how you want to feel. The app matches that description against a catalog of roughly
43,000 titles and returns five, each with a short note on why it fits and where you can watch, read,
or play it.

The matching runs **entirely on your device**. There is no server and no account. The app downloads a
compiled catalog — about 30 MB, rebuilt weekly — and searches it locally, which is why the words you
type never leave your phone.

## What it does not do

- No account, no sign-in, no email address.
- No advertising, no subscription, no in-app purchases.
- No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDK reporting on you.
- No prices. A weekly catalog cannot keep prices accurate, so the app shows availability only —
  whether a title streams, rents, sells, or is free with ads.

## Where the data comes from

Vibezzzz displays metadata from public APIs, cached into a static catalog that is rebuilt weekly. The
app makes **no per-search request** to any of them: it queries only its own local copy. Attribution
for each source is displayed inside the app.

| Media | Source |
|---|---|
| Movies and TV | [The Movie Database (TMDB)](https://www.themoviedb.org/) — titles, release metadata, artwork, and streaming availability. TMDB's watch-provider data is supplied by [JustWatch](https://www.justwatch.com/). |
| Video games | [IGDB](https://www.igdb.com/) |
| Books | [Google Books](https://books.google.com/) |

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

## How it pays for itself

Vibezzzz is free, carries no advertising, and sells nothing. When you choose to open a title on a
streaming service or in a store, that link may earn a commission.

**Title rankings are never affected by commissions.** Results are ordered by how well a title matches
your description and by its quality — nothing else. This is a design constraint, not a policy
statement: see the [affiliate disclosure](disclosure/) for exactly what commercial relationships do
and do not influence.
