> **The short version: Vibezzzz collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, and no
> server of ours to send anything to. What you type never leaves your phone.**

This policy covers the Vibezzzz Android app and this website. It is written to describe the app that
is actually shipped, and it is updated whenever that changes.

## We operate no servers and gather no personal data

Vibezzzz has no backend. We do not run a service that receives your data, so there is nothing for us
to store, profile, sell, or hand over. Specifically, the app does not collect or transmit:

- your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials — it has no accounts;
- any device, advertising, or installation identifier;
- your location;
- your contacts, photos, files, or calendar;
- **your search text**, in any form.

We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not have any. There is no data-broker
relationship, no ad network, and no analytics provider in this app.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything the app remembers is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

- your onboarding quiz answers and settings;
- your recently viewed and recently searched items, if you have that history enabled;
- the downloaded catalog database and cached artwork.

None of it is backed up to us, because there is nowhere to back it up to. Clearing the app's data or
uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently.

## Your searches stay on the device

Search is the part people reasonably worry about, so to be exact: your description is converted into a
numeric representation and compared against the app's local catalog, entirely on your phone's
processor. No part of the search — not the text, not the numeric form of it, not the results — is
sent anywhere.

## The only network requests the app makes

The app talks to exactly three kinds of destination, and never to any of them with your query:

1. **The catalog download.** The app periodically fetches its catalog from GitHub Releases
   (`github.com` / `objects.githubusercontent.com`). This is an anonymous download of the same file
   every user receives.
2. **Artwork.** Posters and backdrops load from TMDB's image CDN (`image.tmdb.org`).
3. **A link you tap.** When you choose to open a title on a streaming service or in a store, the app
   opens that destination in your browser or in that service's own app.

As with any request over the internet, the host you are connecting to can see your IP address and
general technical details of the connection. Those hosts — GitHub, TMDB, and whichever service you
chose to open — are independent companies operating under their own privacy policies, which govern
what they do with that information. We receive none of it.

Downloading the catalog over a mobile connection requires your explicit consent first, because it is
a sizeable download.

## What an affiliate link carries

Some outbound links earn us a commission (see the [affiliate disclosure](../disclosure/)). For those
links to be credited, the affiliate network needs to recognise which app sent the visit. The link
therefore carries a short tracking label made of three things:

- **which part of the app you tapped from** — for example the "stream" section of the availability
  sheet;
- **one broad mood category**, chosen from a fixed list of around a dozen — for example `cosy` or
  `dark`;
- **the app version**.

That label deliberately never includes your search text, any identifier for you or your device, an
account reference, or a timestamp. Its purpose is to tell us which *screen* leads to a purchase — not
which person, and not which search. A broad mood category is not enough to reconstruct what you typed.

## Crash reporting

The app contains no crash-reporting SDK. If your device reports a crash, that happens at the operating
system level through Google Play, under your Android settings and Google's policies, not through
anything in this app. Those reports contain no user content from Vibezzzz.

## This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub serves them and may log requests, including IP
addresses, as described in the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
We add no cookies, no tags, no analytics, and no third-party fonts or scripts of any kind, and we
receive no visitor data.

## Children

Vibezzzz is not directed to children under 13 and is not enrolled in any children's programme on the
app stores. Because the app collects no personal data at all, it collects none from children either.

## Your control

- Clear the app's stored data, or uninstall it, and nothing of yours remains.
- There is no account to delete and no data-export request to make, because we hold nothing. If you
  would like that confirmed in writing, ask and we will confirm it.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes, this page changes with it, and the "last updated" date above changes
too. A change that affected what the app transmits would be announced in the app's release notes.
